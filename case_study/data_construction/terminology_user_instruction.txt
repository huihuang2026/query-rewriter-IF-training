For Insta360 support searches, normalize everyday symptom descriptions to the professional fault terminology below when the provided conversation supports the match. Include the relevant professional term alongside a short description of the customer's actual symptom. Return only the search query.

Use the same terminology guide for every conversation. Select only relevant terms; do not list the catalogue in the query. Keep the product, platform, affected media or screen, confirmed diagnostic answers, and unsuccessful troubleshooting steps when they are present in the provided context.

A professional fault term is a retrieval label, not a confirmed diagnosis. Do not invent a camera model, an intermittent symptom, a seam location, a cause, or information from outside the provided conversation. If the wording is ambiguous, preserve the observed symptom and qualify the professional term as a possible match rather than asserting a fault. If no term fits, keep the customer's description. Do not turn the query into an answer or a repair recommendation.

Context-dependent mapping guide, based on the supplied Customer Description / Issue Type / Professional Fault Term table:
- A black line, stripe or band in the middle of an image or video can be described as "stripe/band of screen". Keep whether the line is in a preview, recorded media or exported media only when the customer has confirmed it. Do not infer flickering from a static line.
- A black line or shadow specifically at the stitching seam can use "Black Shadow at the Seam". A centre-image line alone does not establish that it is at a seam.
- Flashing or flickering on the display can use "Screen Flicker". The words "black line" alone are insufficient to select this term, even though the table contains a broad matching description.
- When the phone can find the camera's Wi-Fi but the app cannot connect, use "Can detect WiFi, but unable to connect to mobile APP". A confirmed connection to the camera's Wi-Fi with continued app failure supports the same term. Seeing a network is not proof that the phone is connected to it.
- When the camera Wi-Fi network cannot be found at all, use "Unable to Detect Wi-Fi Signal". When a previously established app connection repeatedly drops, use "WiFi disconnect with app". Do not replace app-connection failure with either of these different symptoms.
- If the conversation only establishes a general app-connection failure, "Cannot connect to the App" is available without assuming whether Wi-Fi is visible or connected.
- Distinguish "Unable to detect Bluetooth" from "Can detect bluetooth but unable to connect to it" using the diagnostic answers.
- Distinguish a computer that cannot recognize the camera ("Unable to Recognize Camera when Connected to Computer"), transfer trouble ("Abnormal data transmission when connecting to computer"), and a format prompt ("Prompt to format when connected to computer").
